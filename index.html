Most trade businesses lose time and margin in the same few spots. Scan the list. Be honest.

  • You're quoting at 9pm because that's the only quiet time you get.
  • Invoices go out a week (or two) after the job's done — and then you chase them for another two.
  • Variations happen on site but never make it onto the final invoice. That's margin walking out the door.
  • Your leading hand is the only one who actually knows how the business runs. If he's off, it wobbles.
  • Job sheets, receipts, and timesheets live in utes, phones, and text messages — not in one system.
  • You want to step off the tools but the whole thing falls over the minute you try.
  • Apprentices get taught differently depending on whichever tradie they're with that week.

Straight Answers

FAQs — The stuff tradies actually ask

Before you book a chat, here's what most owners want to know up front.

I'm probably too small for this, aren't I?

Probably not. If you've got yourself plus one or two on the tools, there's already enough moving parts to untangle. Small doesn't mean simple — in fact, small businesses usually lose the most margin to messy processes because one lost invoice is a bigger deal.

How much of my time is this going to eat?

A few structured sessions, not weeks of workshops. We work around site hours — early mornings, late arvos, or whenever works. We're not here to slow your business down; we're here to tighten it up.

Do I have to stop working while you do this?

Nope. The business keeps running. We work in parallel with what's already happening — no downing tools, no "transformation projects" that take six months.

What if my numbers aren't all tidy?

Join the club — that's 90% of trade businesses we see. We can work with what you've got. If the numbers are messy, that's usually one of the first things we help you tidy up.

Are you going to try to sell me software?

No. We're tool-agnostic. If your current system works, we'll make it work harder. If it doesn't, we'll help you pick one that fits — but we don't take kickbacks from software vendors, and we've got no reason to push you onto anything specific.

Is this really built for my trade?

We've worked across construction, industrial, and operations for a decade. Plumbers, sparkies, builders, landscapers, service trades — the underlying workflow mess looks pretty similar. The specific fixes get tailored to your trade and how you actually run jobs.

What does it cost?

Depends on scope — crew size, job volume, and what you want sorted. We scope and price every engagement upfront, so you know exactly what you're signing up for before you commit. No surprise invoices. That's a promise.

What if I book a chat and it's not the right fit?

Then we'll say so. If we're not the right people for your situation, we'll tell you — and where we can, we'll point you at someone who might be. No hard sell, no pressure. The 20-minute chat costs you nothing except a coffee's worth of time.
