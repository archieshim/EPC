Straight Answers

FAQs — The stuff owners actually ask

Before you book a chat, here's what most owners want to know up front.

I'm probably too small for this, aren't I?

Probably not. If you've got a handful of people relying on you to keep things running, there's already enough moving parts to untangle. Small doesn't mean simple — smaller businesses often lose the most, proportionally, to messy processes, because one dropped ball is a bigger deal.

How much of my time is this going to eat?

A few structured sessions, not weeks of workshops. We work around your schedule. We're not here to slow your business down; we're here to tighten it up.

Do I have to stop working while you do this?

Nope. The business keeps running. We work in parallel with what's already happening — no downing tools, no "transformation projects" that take six months.

What if my numbers aren't all tidy?

Join the club — that's most of the businesses we see. We can work with what you've got. If the numbers are messy, that's usually one of the first things we help you tidy up.

Are you going to try to sell me software?

No. We're tool-agnostic. If your current system works, we'll make it work harder. If it doesn't, we'll help you pick one that fits — but we don't take kickbacks from software vendors, and we've got no reason to push you onto anything specific.

Is this really built for my industry?

We've worked across construction, industrial, and operations for a decade, and we're now bringing that same approach to professional and financial services firms too. The underlying workflow mess looks pretty similar wherever it shows up — the specific fixes get tailored to how you actually run your business.

What does it cost?

Depends on scope — team size, workload, and what you want sorted. We scope and price every engagement upfront, so you know exactly what you're signing up for before you commit. No surprise invoices. That's a promise.

What if I book a chat and it's not the right fit?

Then we'll say so. If we're not the right people for your situation, we'll tell you — and where we can, we'll point you at someone who might be. No hard sell, no pressure. The 20-minute chat costs you nothing except a coffee's worth of time.
