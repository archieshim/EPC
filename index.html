Straight answers

The stuff owners actually ask

How is EPC different from a business coaching program?

Coaching programs teach you a method and leave the implementation to you. We do the opposite: we analyse your actual data and processes, design a solution specific to your business, and build it with your team — including the systems and automation. You're buying an outcome, not a course.

What data do you need — and what if mine is messy?

Whatever you already have: CRM or practice-management exports, job records, timesheets, invoices, even spreadsheets. Messy is normal — that's most businesses we see. Tidying the data is often one of the first quick wins.

I'm probably too small for this, aren't I?

Probably not. If a handful of people rely on you to keep things running, there are enough moving parts to untangle. Smaller businesses often lose the most, proportionally, to messy processes — one dropped ball is a bigger deal.

How much of my time will this take?

A few structured sessions, not weeks of workshops. We work around your schedule and the business keeps running the whole time — no downing tools, no six-month "transformation".

Are you going to try to sell me software?

No. We're tool-agnostic. If your current system works, we'll make it work harder. If it doesn't, we'll help you pick one that fits — and we don't take kickbacks from vendors.

Do you use AI?

Where it genuinely saves time — drafting, summarising, classifying, data clean-up. But AI is a tool we sometimes use, never the product. We're process consultants first.

Is this built for my industry?

We've worked across construction, industrial and operations for a decade, and now bring the same approach to professional and financial services firms. The workflow mess looks similar everywhere — the fix is tailored to how you run.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope — team size, workload and what you want sorted. We scope and price every engagement upfront, so you know exactly what you're signing up for. No surprise invoices.

What if I book a chat and it's not the right fit?

Then we'll say so — and where we can, point you to someone better suited. No hard sell. The 20-minute chat costs you nothing but a coffee's worth of time.
